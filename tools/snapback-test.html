Snapback tester

Push a stick all the way in one direction and let go, without guiding it back. Repeat in different directions. Each release is recorded for 150 ms. If the stick bounces past the deadzone on the opposite side, that release shows snapback.

Settings

Set the deadzone to what your game uses. Changing it re-scores every recorded release. A release only counts when the stick reaches the deadzone within 40 ms of leaving the flick ring, so slowly guiding it back is ignored.
